Welcome to Our Global Dance Home

This is an international academy open to everyone—whether child or adult, we got you! At Blooms in Motion Dance Academy, we love our students to gain confidence, show their love and passion for dance, and connect with a supportive global community.

Our professional, friendly, and kind teachers are focused and determined to help you master your preferred dance genre, improve your technique, and build lasting friendships across borders.

Explore Our Dance Genres

Choose from our core structured styles, or request any custom genre tailored to your personal goals:

Ballet Classical form characterized by grace, precision, and strict technical vocabulary (e.g., Vaganova, Cecchetti).
Contemporary Expressive style combining elements of modern, jazz, lyrical, and classical ballet, focusing on versatility and improvisation.
Modern Dance A rebellion against classical ballet, emphasizing natural movement, floor work, and the contraction/release of muscles.
Jazz High-energy dance featuring syncopated rhythms, dramatic flair, isolation movements, and dynamic leaps and turns.
Hip-Hop Street dance styles primarily performed to hip-hop music, including breaking (breakdancing), popping, locking, and krumping.
Street Dance & Commercial Urban and club dance styles adapted for music videos, stage performances, and commercial media.
Tap Dance A style where dancers wear shoes fitted with metal heels and toes, using precise foot strikes to create intricate percussive rhythms.
Ballroom (Standard / Smooth) Formal partner dances focused on elegance and sweeping movement across the floor, including the Waltz, Tango, Foxtrot, and Viennese Waltz.
Latin American / Rhythm High-energy partner dances characterized by sensual hip movements and rhythm, including Salsa, Bachata, Cha-Cha-Cha, Rumba, Samba, Jive, and Paso Doble.
Folk & Traditional Cultural dances passed down through generations reflecting regional history, customs, and community identity (e.g., Irish stepdance, Tinikling, Flamenco, Bharatanatyam).
Lyrical Dance A fusion of ballet and jazz that interprets the lyrics and emotional depth of a song through fluid, expressive movement.
Acrobatic Dance (Acro) Combines classical dance technique with precision acrobatic elements, emphasizing strength, flexibility, and tumbling.
K-Pop Cover Dance Precise, sharp choreography replicating music video performances by popular Korean pop groups.
Swing & Social Partner dances originating from the swing era and social dance halls, such as Lindy Hop, Charleston, and East/West Coast Swing.
Breakdancing (Breaking) Athletic street dance consisting of four primary elements: toprock, downrock, power moves, and freezes.
Dancehall Jamaican street dance rooted in Caribbean culture, featuring dynamic grooves and social dances.
Vogue & Waacking Styles born out of the LGBTQ+ ballroom scene and disco era, characterized by sharp arm movements, dramatic posing, and runway-inspired lines.
Belly Dance (Raqs Sharqi) Middle Eastern folk and classical dance forms emphasizing complex abdominal isolations, fluid torso movements, and rhythmic hip work.
Polynesian & Hula Traditional storytelling dances from Hawaii, Tahiti, and surrounding islands that use hand motions and footwork to narrate legends or nature.
Flamenco Expressive Spanish art form consisting of guitar playing, song, and passionate dance featuring rhythmic hand clapping and foot stomping.
...and more genres according to student preferences! We are here to help you learn what you love.

Why Choose Us?

Our online private classes are designed to reduce hassle so you can still make time for your passion without conflicting with your busy schedule. Our professional teachers are focused and determined to help you love dance more—they are friendly, kind, professional, and respectful. We not only value learning dancing, but also nurturing good relationships to improve student skills, gain friends, and build a true family.

Class Format & Requirements

  • 1-on-1 Online Video Classes: Sessions are strictly between the student and teacher to focus heavily on proper technique and receive direct verbal guidance.
  • Duration: Standard classes are 1 hour, but can be extended based on scheduling availability.
  • Technical Setup: Students must have their own reliable device, stable Wi-Fi connection, and a private room to avoid distractions during class.
